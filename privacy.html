Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 13, 2026

I Me Mine is an English grammar practice app. This page describes the intended privacy approach and will be finalized against the release build before App Store publication.

Practice and progress

The app stores practice history and progress on your device using local storage. It does not require you to create an account.

In-app purchases

Optional Plus purchases are processed by Apple through the App Store. Apple handles payment information; I Me Mine does not collect credit card details. Purchase status is checked to unlock Plus features.

Data sharing

The app does not intentionally sell personal information or use third-party advertising trackers. Apple may process purchase-related information under its own privacy policy.

Your choices

You can remove locally stored practice data by deleting the app. You can manage purchases through your Apple account.

Contact

For privacy questions, please visit the support page.

This policy should be reviewed against the final shipped app and its App Store privacy disclosures.
